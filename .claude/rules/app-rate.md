# An ASIO app at its own sample rate (dasio#107)

- The Dante rate is read-only (owner, 13.11.2026): the device stays at 96 kHz; an app at 44.1 or 48 kHz (`shm::APP_RATES`) is converted in the device process, never in the driver.
- The path: driver `setSampleRate` -> `requested_rate` in `Global\dasio-<n>-dir` -> the device's 100 ms loop -> `run::serve::Base::serve(Plan)` re-serves in process (new section, new pump; the Dante device runs on). The directory outlives sessions, so a device restarted under a DAW comes back at the DAW's rate.
- The processor: `Bridge` alone at 96 kHz (bit-identical to before); `dlink::rated::Rated::with_fixed_app_block(Bridge, ..)` otherwise (dstack#330; dasio's own reblock went in 0.1.105). ASIO needs a fixed buffer: Rated gives the bridge `A = ceil(B * R / 96000)` frames a call, 0 or 1 calls a device block. 48 kHz on an even block is exact (half-band, one DAW block per device block, no prime); 44.1 kHz waits in Rated's RX FIFO and adds a TX prime of one DAW block. `Served::new` refuses a Rated whose `app_block()` is not `Plan::app_block`. The bridge writes nothing with no DAW or deep in a gap, so its outputs must start at silence each call: since dstack v0.6.151 (dstack issue 335) Rated zeroes the app's outputs before each call, as the pump does, and dasio's own `Zeroed` wrapper is gone (0.1.108). Test `a_block_the_bridge_leaves_alone_plays_silence_on_either_path` holds dstack to it at 96/48/44.1 kHz; if it fails after a dstack move, the replayed DAW block is back: report it to dstack, never re-add a local wrapper silently.
- The bridge runs in app samples: its block starts, its deadline clock (`to_samples(ns, app_rate)`) and its offset (`Plan::wait_offset`, rounded down) are all at the app's rate. Its wait counts from the DEVICE block's start (`bridge::WaitFrom`), not the DAW block's: `Served`'s converted path sets it to `Plan::at_app(device start)` (rounded down, the device block's start on the bridge's own clock) BEFORE Rated runs that device block, since Rated hands the DAW a block that starts up to A-1 frames earlier. The stamp cannot live inside Rated (the inner block cannot know how many frames waited before it). `Served::new` takes the bridge's `wait_from()`.
- A change to the directory or header layout bumps `shm::VERSION` and `install.ps1`'s `$ShmProtocol` together (shm's test checks they agree); `Shape::new` keeps the pump's blocks, `Shape::app` the app's.
- Reported latencies: `Plan::latencies` = the device's terms at R + the free `dlink::rated::delay_ns` (converters + dlink's PRIME) + the fixed block's share `Plan::fixed_block_share` (A/2 in, A - A/2 out on 44.1, none on 48; their sum is the prime the fixed block adds, pinned against `Rated::delay_ns` in a test). Never report the fixed Rated's own `delay_ns()`: its RX is the worst case (A-1), which overstates the round trip by up to a block. The pulse test in `app_rate/tests.rs` measures the real path against it.
- Tests run the converter as it ships: `Cargo.toml`'s `[profile.dev.package.*]` overrides build rubato, dstack-resample, dlink and the audioadapter crates with `opt-level = 3` AND `debug-assertions = false`, `overflow-checks = false`. Opt-level alone is not enough: debug assertions turn on std's unsafe-precondition checks inside rubato's AVX loop (111-183 us a block at the e2e shape; the coverage job's 44.1 kHz step overran). A new crate on the converter's hot path gets the same three lines. `bench_bridge.rs` holds the conversion's own cost at the e2e shape under a quarter of the device block.
