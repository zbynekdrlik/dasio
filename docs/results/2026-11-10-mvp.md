# MVP results (plan Task 10)

The numbers that prove the MVP of `docs/plans/2026-10-29-dante-asio-mvp-plan.md`
(Task 10, steps 2–6), and what the PCs run since. Numbers only: no addresses,
device or channel names (dasio's rule). Each line cites the dasio issue comment
it comes from (issue, comment time UTC). All runs are at 96 kHz.

Terms: B is the ASIO block, P the device's period, W the work margin, "spare"
the extra block the DAW gets (`--spare-blocks 1`). "Late" counts blocks the
DAW answered after the device needed them; "missed" counts blocks that went out
as silence.
